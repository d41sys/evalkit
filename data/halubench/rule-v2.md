What counts as faithful:
- Conclusions, interpretations and hedged implications that a careful reader would draw from the findings in the DOCUMENT (for example "suggests", "may", "provides a rationale for", "highlights a role for") are faithful. New facts that the DOCUMENT does not contain are not.
- The ANSWER must answer the QUESTION with what the DOCUMENT says. An answer that gives a different, merely related statement instead of the fact asked for is not faithful, even if that statement appears in the DOCUMENT.
- A short answer may be several answers joined with commas, often repeating the same value (for example "14, 14"). Treat repeated values as one answer, and judge the values, not the format.
- Do not fail an answer for dropping a qualifier, for slightly stronger or weaker wording of something the DOCUMENT says, for combining facts from different parts of the DOCUMENT, or for a fair summary of it.
